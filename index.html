Why Homeowners Call Us

Your home is only as strong
as its weakest opening.

During a hurricane, if one window fails, wind rushes into the house and creates tremendous pressure inside — while the storm is pulling on the other windows, doors and roof from outside. Pushed from inside and pulled from outside, more windows and doors can blow out, and the uplift on your roof goes way up. Your ceiling sheetrock isn't structural — it isn't designed to hold your roof down.

Windows And Roof Work Together

Impact windows help keep the building envelope intact, while a properly installed roof system is designed to resist hurricane-force uplift. One without the other leaves a gap.

Protect the openings, protect the roof — protect the whole home

Your Power Bill in August

Old single-pane windows let the cool air you paid for leak straight outside, and the sun fades your floors and furniture while it happens. New impact glass keeps the house cooler and quieter — you notice it every day, not just during a storm.

Most homeowners notice the difference the first month

Insurance Getting Harder

More Florida homeowners are getting rate hikes, or a letter saying they won't be renewed, because of the age of their roof or unprotected windows. Insurers look hard at your roof — not just your windows — so both matter when it's time to renew.

A new roof and impact openings — both count

A Small Leak, A Big Bill

Florida has a rule that once repairs cover more than a quarter of a section of roof, that whole section has to be brought up to today's code. On an older home, a patch job can turn into a much bigger one — and you deserve to know that before anyone starts, not after.

We put repair-or-replace in writing, with the reason

Straight Answers

Questions we get
every week.

Will impact windows really lower my insurance?

Usually, yes. Florida insurers give a discount for protecting the windows and doors, and it's one of the bigger discounts on a policy. After the work is done and inspected, an inspector fills out a form for your insurance company. How much you save depends on your policy and your roof, and covering every window and door earns more than covering some of them. We'll be straight with you about what to expect rather than promising a number we can't control.

What is PACE, and would I qualify?

PACE is a program that pays for a roof, windows or doors, and you pay it back through your property tax bill instead of a regular loan. We're Ygrene Certified, and PACE approval isn't based on your credit score. It isn't offered everywhere and each program has its own rules, so we check your address first and walk you through it.

What kind of windows and doors do you install?

High impact rated windows and doors, in every style and shape your house has. We work with more than one manufacturer, so we can pick the right product for your house and your budget instead of forcing one brand on every job.

My roof only leaks in one spot. Do I need a whole new roof?

Maybe not — but there's a Florida rule that decides it, not us. Once repairs cover more than about a quarter of a section of roof in a year, that whole section has to be brought up to today's code. On an older house, once we open it up we sometimes find soft wood underneath that pushes the job over that line. We'll show you what we find, put repair-or-replace in writing with the reason, and let you decide before anything is torn off.

How long does the whole thing take?

The waiting is mostly on the window and door order, not at your house. Once everything is measured, permitted and built, most homes are done in one to three days depending on how many windows there are and whether there's stucco work around them. We give you a real date up front and tell you right away if it moves.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — and you shouldn't take our word for it. Windows Roofs Plus Inc. holds Florida Certified Roofing Contractor license CCC1333631 and Broward County Structural Carpentry Specialty Contractor license 21-CRP-22416-X, carries liability and workers' comp, and is BBB Accredited with an A+ rating. You can look the license up yourself at myfloridalicense.com, and you should do that with any contractor who knocks on your door.

What kind of warranty do I get?

The windows, doors and roofing materials all come with the manufacturer's warranty. Our own workmanship warranty is written into your contract in plain language — ask us for it, read it, and ask anyone else you're getting a quote from for theirs too. If a contractor won't put a warranty in writing, that tells you something.
