Your home is only as strong
as its weakest opening.

Broward County sits inside Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. Wind doesn't take a house apart from the outside — it finds one failed opening.

Florida Sun & Salt

UV intensity and salt air age exterior materials faster here than almost anywhere in the country. Old single-pane glass bleeds conditioned air; a tired roof loses granules and fastener grip long before it visibly leaks.

Once more than a quarter of a roof section is repaired inside twelve months, Florida code generally requires the whole section be brought current. On older homes, a leak repair crosses that line the moment the surface opens.

Straight Answers

Questions we get
every week.

Do impact windows actually lower my homeowners insurance?

In most cases, yes. Florida carriers price wind-mitigation credits into premiums, and opening protection is one of the largest single credits available. Once the windows and doors are installed and permitted, a wind-mitigation inspection documents the upgrade for your carrier. How much you save depends on your policy, your roof, and how much of the home is protected — covering every opening generally earns more than covering some.

What is PACE financing, and does my home qualify?

P.A.C.E. (Property Assessed Clean Energy) funds qualifying resilience upgrades — roofing, impact windows, impact doors — and is repaid as an assessment on your property tax bill rather than a conventional loan payment. Approval leans on home equity and ability to repay rather than a credit-score minimum, terms commonly run up to 20 years, and many programs require no money down. Availability depends on your county and city, so we confirm eligibility for your exact address before anything is signed.

Are your windows and doors made in Florida?

Yes. Our impact windows and doors are manufactured in Florida and installed factory direct. That means shorter lead times, product engineered to Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone standards rather than a generic national spec, and one less layer of markup between the plant and your home.

My roof only leaks in one spot. Do I need a whole new roof?

Not necessarily — but Florida's 25% rule decides it, not preference. Once more than a quarter of a roof section is repaired or replaced within a twelve-month period, that section generally has to be brought up to current code. On older homes, opening the surface often reveals soft decking or failed underlayment that pushes the job past the threshold. We put the repair-versus-replace call in writing, with the reason, before any tear-off begins.

How long does an impact window installation take?

Manufacturing lead time is the long pole, not install day. Once your order is measured, permitted and built, a typical single-family home is installed in one to three days depending on opening count and whether stucco or trim work is involved. We give you the real schedule at estimate time and update you if the plant's queue moves.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Windows Roofs Plus Inc. holds Florida Certified Roofing Contractor license CCC1333631 and carries liability and workers' compensation coverage. We're BBB Accredited with an A+ rating. You can verify our license at any time at myfloridalicense.com — and you should verify any contractor you're considering.

What warranty comes with the work?

Impact windows and doors carry the manufacturer's warranty on the product itself, and roofing materials carry their manufacturer's coverage. Workmanship coverage is stated explicitly in your written contract — ask for it in writing from us or from anyone else you're quoting, and read what it does and doesn't cover.
